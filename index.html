Frequently Asked Questions

What is BMI?

BMI is a value calculated from weight and height. It is used as a general adult screening measure.

Is BMI a medical diagnosis?

No. BMI should be considered with other health factors and is not intended to diagnose disease.

Is BMI accurate for athletes?

BMI may be less accurate for very muscular people because muscle mass can increase body weight.

Can children use this calculator?

This calculator is for adults aged 20 and above. Children and teenagers require age- and sex-specific BMI percentiles.
